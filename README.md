## 📊 Key Financial KPIs

### Revenue

Measures the company's overall sales/revenue performance.

### Gross Profit

```text
Gross Profit = Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold
```

### Gross Margin

```text
Gross Margin % = Gross Profit / Revenue × 100
```

### EBITDA

```text
EBITDA = Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation & Amortization
```

### EBITDA Margin

```text
EBITDA Margin % = EBITDA / Revenue × 100
```

### Net Profit

Measures the profit remaining after expenses, interest and taxes.
